Interview Profile

Q1

Tell us about Clement.

Clement is a warm, inquisitive 4-year-old who approaches every new experience with enthusiasm and an open mind. He is gentle with others, communicates well for his age, and adapts quickly to new environments.

Q2

What are Clement's strengths?

He is a strong communicator who expresses himself clearly. He demonstrates early problem-solving skills through play, shows creativity in art and storytelling, and has a natural empathy for those around him.

Q3

How does Clement interact with others?

Clement is sociable and makes friends easily. He enjoys collaborative play, takes turns willingly, and is considerate of his peers' feelings. He listens attentively to adults and follows instructions well.
